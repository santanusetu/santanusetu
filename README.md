## What I've built

**Payments fraud & risk platforms**

- 📜 **Rule management platform** - rebuilt the real-time decisioning workflow; rule lookup went from 8 minutes to 12 seconds across 800+ active rules.
- 📊 **Fraud reporting & analytics suite** - customer-facing analytics kept off the latency-critical authorization path; cut support tickets by 62%.
- 🔭 **SLA observability** - coverage across 8.34M+ monthly requests; SLA-miss detection went from 3 weeks to under a day.
- 🧩 **React micro-frontend migration** - moved 85+ Angular pages to React and Module Federation with zero production incidents.

**Agentic AI & security**

- 🔌 **Enterprise MCP server** - exposes rule, KPI and reporting data to AI agents as permission-scoped tools; adopted by 6 teams.
- 🛡️ **Agentic security remediation** - a framework that closed 130 of 130 SAST findings and produced 19 reusable fix patterns, now standard across 40+ repositories.
- ⚙️ **Analyst workflow automation** - per-rule processing went from 2 hours to 4 minutes, saving about 9,000 analyst-hours a year.
